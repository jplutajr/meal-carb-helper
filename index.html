Meal Carb Helper

Safety: This app estimates the carbs to enter into your MiniMed 780G. It can be wrong. Verify before dosing. Your pump/SmartGuard calculates the actual insulin dose.
MiniMed 780G workflow:
Main output = carbs to enter into pump.
Your pump/SmartGuard calculates actual bolus using your settings, sensor/BG, insulin on board, and algorithm behavior.
