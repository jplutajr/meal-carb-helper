Meal Carb Helper

Safety: This app estimates carbs from a photo and calculates a bolus estimate using your saved pump settings. It can be wrong. Always verify before dosing insulin.
Saved pump math:
Carb ratio: 1 unit per 15g carbs
Correction factor: 1 unit lowers 45 mg/dL
Target BG: 100 mg/dL
Bolus rounding: nearest 0.1u
